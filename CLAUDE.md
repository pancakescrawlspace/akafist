This repo holds two independent projects: `akathist/` — the Kazan akathist and a Church Slavonic dictionary of it
(master `akathist/dictionary/dictionary.psv`, rebuilt with `tools/build.py`); and `djachenko/` — a multi-session
project to digitise Дьяченко's 1900 dictionary (`tools/dj_*.py`). The second does not depend on the first.
Before working on the latter, read djachenko/PROGRESS.md (start at its NEXT: line) and djachenko/PLAN.md; end every session by updating PROGRESS.md and committing.
Every copy/scan of the book found so far (downloaded or not) is recorded in djachenko/COPIES.md; update it whenever a source is found or checked.
